SERVICE 02 • CERTIFIED INFRASTRUCTURE

COMMERCIAL LPG GAS &
BOILER STEAM PIPELINE

Gas safety and steam integrity cannot be left to amateur plumbing. VIMAL specializes in high-standard commercial LPG cylinder manifold networks, LOT (Liquid Off-Take) systems, pressure-regulating stations, and insulated steam distribution networks.

PIPELINE DESIGN & FIT

Seamless ASTM A106/A53 Grade carbon steel or SS 304 pipes with forged fittings.

PRESSURE TESTING

Pneumatic and hydrostatic hold test at 1.5x working pressure with test certificate.

AUTOMATIC SAFETY VALVES

Pneumatic slam-shut valves coupled with gas leak detection sensors and sirens.

BOILER STEAM LINES

Thermodynamic steam traps, strainers, and mineral wool insulation for minimal loss.

SERVICE 03 • PREVENTATIVE CARE

ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACTS (AMC)

Unplanned commercial kitchen downtime during peak service hours destroys customer trust and operational revenue. VIMAL's structured AMC programs provide scheduled preventative visits, leak tests, and burner calibrations.

Scheduled Quarterly Audits: Comprehensive physical inspection of burners, ignition pins, pilot lines, and exhaust blowers.
Electronic Gas Leak Scans: Calibrated sniffer checks on all manifold joints, valves, and flexible pigtails.
