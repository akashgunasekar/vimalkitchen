ABOUT VIMAL KITCHEN EQUIPMENT

ENGINEERING RELIABILITY
FOR COMMERCIAL KITCHENS.

We combine heavy-duty stainless steel culinary equipment fabrication with certified LPG gas and boiler steam pipeline engineering to deliver turnkey commercial kitchen infrastructure.

NOT JUST EQUIPMENT.
COMPLETE INFRASTRUCTURE.

VIMAL Kitchen Equipment was established to address the critical gap between equipment suppliers and pipeline contractors in the commercial food service industry. In high-output culinary operations, equipment cannot function safely without calibrated gas manifolds, balanced exhaust ventilation, and reliable steam distribution.

By integrating equipment fabrication with complete utility engineering under one roof, we eliminate coordination failures, ensure safety compliance, and maximize kitchen productivity for hotels, restaurants, industrial canteens, and institutional facilities.
